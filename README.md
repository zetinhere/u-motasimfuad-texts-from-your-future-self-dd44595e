One text message from your future self, written by a small deterministic program for make.fun's "Texts From Your Future Self" bounty. The text: "Future you here: do NOT send that 3am text to your crush. In 2028 she reads it aloud on her podcast to 2 million listeners. Episode one. It's titled after you."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (159 characters, one message).

## How the line was chosen

Eight candidate lines were scored against the published judge brief (future-self voice first, then whether the joke lands, then specific details, then character economy). The full tournament is in [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md). The winner beats the runner-up — an ex marrying the guy who invented your idea — on punchline speed: its payoff is four words, "It's titled after you."
